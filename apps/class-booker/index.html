First-time setup (once, ~2 min)
  1. Chrome autofill — Chrome ⋮ → Settings → Autofill → turn on Addresses (and Payment methods). Fills your name & email at every checkout.
  2. Stripe Link — the first time you book Bettina, save your card to Link. After that, any Stripe checkout recognizes you for one-tap pay.
  3. Google Pay — keep a card in Google Wallet; it appears as a one-tap button at Ballet BC (and Bettina once enabled).
